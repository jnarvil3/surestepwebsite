Automated Lead Prospecting That Saves 15+ Hours of Research Weekly

A Brazilian pet super app startup needed to build targeted prospect lists of local pet businesses for outreach, but their team was spending days manually searching Google Maps, visiting websites, and copying contact info into spreadsheets.

The Solution

An End-to-End Lead Enrichment Pipeline

We built an end-to-end pipeline that searches Google Maps for businesses matching target criteria, crawls their websites to extract emails and contact details, scores and ranks each lead, and delivers a clean, enriched list ready for outreach.
